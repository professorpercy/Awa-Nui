AWA NUI
AWA NUI is a music discovery platform organised by space — where it came from, what it connects to. How it works: Each node is an album. Colour = genre. Position = geographic origin or genre cluster. Lines connect albums that share DNA. Click a node to explore it. Personal Garden = your record collection, your favorite albums. Global Garden = the collective map. Switch between genre view and country view, use the ticker bar to filter.
